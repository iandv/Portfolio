# Glitch-Out
A third person shooter inspired by Tomb Raider and Half-Life where you play as a robot.

![1_Main Menu](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e615bae3-c802-417e-b7b2-d452dd652e4f)

## 🚀 Features
- Modular damage system for limbs.
- Amputation of limbs and debuff when limb's health reaches a threshold.
- Upgrade system where you spend points to improve your weapons.
- Health terminal where you spend the terminal's stored health in your different limbs to heal them.
- Enemy AI using decision trees.
